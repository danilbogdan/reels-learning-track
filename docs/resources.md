## Books

| Book | What for |
|---|---|
| Austin Kleon, Steal Like an Artist | taking other people's forms and reworking them as your own |
| Austin Kleon, Show Your Work! | sharing the process, not only the result |
| Brendan Kane, Hook Point | the first seconds of a video |
| Chip Heath, Dan Heath, Made to Stick | why some ideas stick |
| Jonah Berger, Contagious | why people share content |
| Carmine Gallo, Talk Like TED | structure and delivery |
| Michael Port, Steal the Show | performing as a craft |
| Paul Jarvis, Company of One | a small, durable business instead of chasing scale |

The books were not checked against their texts; these are recommendations from memory.

## Reading order

Loose suggestion; see also the [course schedule](course/schedule.md#reading-alongside-optional-loose).

1. Austin Kleon, *Steal Like an Artist* (start here)
2. Chip and Dan Heath, *Made to Stick*
3. Austin Kleon, *Show Your Work!*
4. Jonah Berger, *Contagious*

The rest of the list can wait. The list was compiled from memory and not checked against the texts; find editions and translations through your library or bookstore.
