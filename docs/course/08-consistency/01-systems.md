# Lesson 8.1: Systems for consistency

**English** | [Русский](../../ru/course/08-consistency/01-systems.md)

**Time:** 40 minutes

## Goal

Build a system that makes showing up the default, protects you from burnout and keeps your content going after the first month.

## Why systems beat motivation

Motivation is high at the start and fades. The creators who last are the ones with a system that works on low-energy days. A system lowers the cost of each reel so that consistency needs less willpower.

## The four components

### 1. A weekly rhythm

Assign tasks to days so you don't decide every day.

| Day | Task |
|---|---|
| Monday | Weekly review, choose three concepts, write beat sheets |
| Tuesday | Film session (batch) |
| Wednesday | Edit |
| Thursday | Post, respond to comments |
| Friday | Light day: voice notes, reference study |
| Weekend | Rest or optional bonus content |

Adjust to your life. The point is that each day has a default.

### 2. A minimum day

Define the smallest action that keeps the streak: a voice note. On days when everything falls apart, do it and stop. Resist the temptation to raise the minimum when you feel good, so it stays easy.

### 3. A content bank

Maintain a bank of reels that are filmed or ready to film, so you never face an empty week.

* Keep at least **two finished reels in reserve** once you are posting.
* Keep at least **ten concepts** in your idea inbox at any time.
* When you feel creative, fill the bank, not just the schedule.

### 4. Templates and presets

Reduce decisions by building reusable pieces:

* Editing project template with captions and export preset
* Caption style
* Cover style
* A camera recipe (lesson 5.1)
* Beat-sheet templates for each format
* A quality checklist (lesson 6.4)

Every template is one less choice on a tired day.

## Time budget

Be honest about time. If you have five hours a week, plan for five hours, not twenty.

A sample budget for two reels a week:

| Activity | Time |
|---|---|
| Ideas and beat sheets | 45 min |
| Filming (batch) | 60 min |
| Editing | 90 min |
| Posting and comments | 30 min |
| Review and logging | 30 min |
| Voice notes and drills | 35 min |
| **Total** | **~5 hours** |

If you cannot sustain it, reduce the frequency before you reduce the quality.

## Spotting burnout

Signs: dread when you open the camera, resentment of the audience, obsessive checking of stats, skipping rest, losing interest in the topic.

What helps:

* **Reduce frequency** for a few weeks, but keep the minimum day.
* **Return to play:** make something silly or personal with no plan to publish.
* **Change one element:** location, format, time.
* **Talk to someone** about it.
* **Take real rest:** a day without content.
* **Revisit why you started.**

Planned breaks are better than collapse. Announce a short pause or post from the bank.

## Accountability

Tell one person your plan and check in weekly. Or join a small group of people with similar goals. Public commitment can help, but keep it kind; don't tie your self-worth to your streak.

## Protect your life

Content about yourself can start to take over your life: experiences become material, and you may feel pressure to perform. Keep some experiences undocumented. Protect relationships, health and work. A reel is never worth damaging them.

## Sustainability checklist

* [ ] My week has a rhythm.
* [ ] I have a minimum day I can always do.
* [ ] I have at least ten concepts and two reels in reserve.
* [ ] I have templates for my common tasks.
* [ ] My time budget is realistic.
* [ ] I know my burnout signs and my response.

## Common mistakes

* **Planning a pace you cannot hold.**
* **No reserve:** every week starts from zero.
* **Skipping rest.**
* **Treating a missed day as failure.** Resume the next day; never double up.
* **Letting content consume your life.**

## Exercise

1. Write your weekly rhythm.
2. Calculate your honest weekly time budget.
3. Create or refine three templates.
4. Write what you will do on a burnout day.

## Daily practice

* **Minimum (keeps the streak):** a voice note of any length.
* **Today's take (about 30 seconds):** Tell the lens what you will do on a bad day to keep the streak.
* Weeks 1-2 everything stays private; from week 3 follow your publishing plan.
* Don't watch the take right away (lesson 4.1). Tick the day in your tracker.

## Checklist

* [ ] My rhythm is on paper.
* [ ] My budget is honest.
* [ ] Templates exist.
* [ ] I have a plan for hard days.
* [ ] I recorded today's voice note and take.
