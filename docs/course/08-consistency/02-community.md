# Lesson 8.2: Comments, collaboration and community

**English** | [Русский](../../ru/course/08-consistency/02-community.md)

**Time:** 35 minutes

## Goal

Treat the people who watch you as people, handle comments well, protect yourself from abuse, and use collaboration to grow.

## Why community matters

Platforms measure reactions, but audiences are not metrics. The people who comment, send your videos to friends and message you are the beginning of a community. They are also your best source of ideas: questions they ask are future reels.

## Comments

### Reply early and with substance

* In the first hour after posting, reply to the first comments. It builds a habit of conversation.
* Reply with more than "thanks": ask a follow-up question, add a detail, use their name.
* Pin a good comment or question if the platform allows it.
* Use comments as a source of ideas: "Great question, I'll make a video about that."

### Turn questions into content

A comment such as "How do you actually start when you don't feel like it?" can become a reel. Replying with a video, where the platform supports it, or answering in the next reel with a nod to the person, shows you listen.

### Handling disagreement

* Not every disagreement is an attack. A respectful challenge can be an opportunity to clarify.
* Respond calmly with facts or your experience, or don't respond.
* If you were wrong, say so briefly. It builds trust.

### Handling abuse and spam

* You do not owe anyone a reply.
* **Delete, hide, block, report.** Use the platform's comment filters to hide offensive words automatically.
* Don't argue with trolls.
* Screenshot and report threats or harassment, and seek help if you feel unsafe.
* If comments affect your mood, step away from the app for a while. Ask a friend to moderate for you if needed.

## DMs and messages

* Keep boundaries. You can ignore or mute messages.
* Watch for scams: offers that need payment, "brand deals" that ask you to pay a fee, phishing links, requests for passwords. Real brands do not ask you to pay to work with them.
* Don't share personal details in DMs with strangers.

## Collaboration

Working with other creators can reach new audiences and make content more interesting.

Ways to collaborate:

* **Duet or stitch-style responses** (where available): react to or build on another person's video, with their permission settings allowing it.
* **Joint videos:** film a conversation or a challenge together.
* **Cross-mentions:** shout out a creator you genuinely like.
* **Guest appearances:** interview someone.
* **Comment exchange:** meaningful, not mechanical.

Start with creators at a similar size. Offer value first: a helpful comment, a share, a thoughtful reply. Ask for permission and agree on credit, usage and timing before filming.

## Audience trust

Trust is the asset that makes monetization later possible.

* **Be consistent** in your voice and values.
* **Be honest** about mistakes and about sponsored content (lesson 9.2).
* **Don't pretend** to be someone you are not.
* **Credit** other people's ideas and work.
* **Protect your audience:** don't promote things you wouldn't use.

## Growing a small community

* Create a recurring segment people can look forward to.
* Invite viewers to share their experience.
* Highlight a viewer's story (with their permission).
* Keep one place, such as a newsletter or group chat, for people who want more. Only if you can maintain it.

## Common mistakes

* **Ignoring comments** while asking people to comment.
* **Arguing with trolls.**
* **Spending hours on comments** at the expense of making things.
* **Collaborating for numbers only.**
* **Falling for brand-deal scams.**

## Exercise

1. After your first post, reply to every comment within 24 hours.
2. Collect three questions from comments or friends. Turn each into an idea card.
3. Set up comment filters and a blocking policy.
4. List five creators you could collaborate with, and one way you might offer them value.

## Checklist

* [ ] I reply to comments and learn from them.
* [ ] I have moderation tools set.
* [ ] I know common scam signs.
* [ ] I have a short list of potential collaborators.
