# Lesson 7.1: Posting mechanics

**English** | [Русский](../../ru/course/07-publishing/01-posting-mechanics.md)

**Time:** 40 minutes

## Goal

Publish your reels with a clear caption, sensible hashtags, a good cover and correct settings, and set up a posting routine that you can keep.

## Before you post: three checks

1. **Privacy check** done (lesson 2.3).
2. **Quality check** done (lesson 6.4).
3. **Disclosure check:** is any part of the video an ad, a partnership, a gifted product or self-promotion? If yes, see lesson 9.2 and use the platform's label before posting.

## Account basics

For Instagram, an account of the **Creator** type is a good fit for personal-brand content. According to a [Buffer overview](https://buffer.com/resources/instagram-account-type/), Creator accounts include analytics, scheduling of posts up to about a month ahead, and the fuller music library. Switch types in the account settings if needed.

For TikTok, the standard account has creator tools; analytics access depends on the account settings.

Check the current options in each app, since menus and names change.

## The caption

The caption has three jobs: add context, help the right people find the video, and invite a response if appropriate.

* **First line:** the part visible before "more". Write it as a second hook or a clear statement of the topic.
* **Body:** short. One to three lines is enough for most reels. Add a detail the video did not cover, or a question.
* **Call to action:** only if it is genuine and specific: "What's your version of this?" works better than "like and follow."
* **Keywords in natural language:** mention the topic in plain words; people search by phrases.

TikTok names captions, sounds and hashtags among the information about a video that its recommendation system uses ([source](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-us/how-tiktok-recommends-videos-for-you/)), so what you write helps the system understand the topic, but it will not rescue a video that people do not watch.

## Hashtags

There is no verified magic number. The role of hashtags is to indicate the topic.

* Use a **small set (3-5)** that describe the video accurately: a mix of broader and narrower topics.
* Avoid unrelated popular tags; they attract the wrong audience and weaken signals about what the video is.
* Avoid banned or spammy tags.
* Test and note: keep a log of the tags you used and the results. Your own data is the only reliable guide (lesson 7.2).

## Cover and title text

* Choose a cover frame that looks good in the profile grid.
* If the platform lets you add cover text, keep it short and readable.
* A consistent look across covers helps profile coherence.

## Audio selection

If you add sound inside the app, follow your account's licensing rules (lesson 6.3). Use original audio when you have spoken content; it is yours.

## Settings to check

* **Who can see:** public, followers or private. Decide before posting.
* **Comments:** on, with filters set if needed.
* **Remix/duet/stitch options:** decide whether others can reuse your video. Turn off if you prefer.
* **Location tag:** leave off unless it serves a purpose and is safe.
* **Alt text / accessibility description:** where offered, add a short description.
* **Commercial content label:** use if relevant. TikTok says that content promoting a brand, product or service requires its content disclosure setting ([TikTok help](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/about-the-content-disclosure-setting-for-creators)); Instagram requires its paid partnership label for organic branded content ([Instagram help](https://help.instagram.com/android-app/1109894795810258)).

## Cross-posting

If you post on both Instagram and TikTok:

* Upload the original clean file to each. Do not download a video from one app and upload it to another, since the logo watermark can reduce reach (Instagram lists watermarks among reach reducers).
* Adjust the caption and hashtags per platform.
* Keep an eye on separate analytics.

For the first 3-4 weeks, consider focusing on one platform to keep the workload small.

## When to post

There is no universal best time. Generic charts are averages of other people's audiences. Start with times when you can respond to comments in the first hour, and then use your analytics to see when your followers are active. Consistency matters more than the exact hour.

## Posting rhythm

* Week 3: three reels spread over the week.
* Week 4: four or five.
* Afterward: choose the frequency you can sustain without burning out. Two reliable reels a week beats seven that stop after a month.

## After you post

* Stay 10-15 minutes and reply to the first comments. It is also a way to learn what people took from the video.
* Do not refresh the stats every minute. Look once at 24 hours, once at 7 days.
* Log: date, title, hook, length, platform, tags, and any settings. This feeds the analysis in lesson 7.2.

## Common mistakes

* **Caption that repeats the video word for word.**
* **A wall of unrelated hashtags.**
* **Forgetting commercial labels** when relevant.
* **Posting and disappearing.**
* **Checking stats obsessively.**

## Exercise

1. Write a caption for your first reel with a strong first line and a question.
2. Pick 3-5 hashtags that describe it.
3. Decide your visibility, comment and remix settings.
4. Post your first reel (week 3 onward). Reply to the first comments.
5. Add a row to your log.

## Daily practice

* **Minimum (keeps the streak):** a voice note of any length.
* **Today's take (about 30 seconds):** Film something for your thread. Write its caption and 3-5 hashtags (publish only if your plan says so).
* Weeks 1-2 everything stays private; from week 3 follow your publishing plan.
* Don't watch the take right away (lesson 4.1). Tick the day in your tracker.

## Reading (optional)

This week's book, about 15-20 minutes: Austin Kleon, *Show Your Work!*. A loose suggestion; it does not have to match the lesson. More in [resources](../../resources.md) and the [schedule](../schedule.md).

## Checklist

* [ ] Privacy, quality and disclosure checks are done.
* [ ] Caption: strong first line, short body.
* [ ] 3-5 relevant hashtags.
* [ ] Settings reviewed.
* [ ] I logged the post.
* [ ] I recorded today's voice note and take.
