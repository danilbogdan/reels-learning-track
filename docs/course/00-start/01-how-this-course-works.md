# Lesson 0.1: How this course works

**English** | [Русский](../../ru/course/00-start/01-how-this-course-works.md)

**Time:** 30-40 minutes

## Goal

By the end of this lesson you know what you are committing to, you have a working setup, and you have recorded your first (bad, private) take.

## What this course is

This is a 30-day course in making short vertical video about yourself: your life, your work, your ideas. It is built around one belief: **the bottleneck is not knowledge, it is the habit of filming**. Most people who want to make reels read a lot, film a little, and quit in a week or two. So the course front-loads habit and leaves publishing for later.

The structure:

* **Weeks 1-2: build the habit and the raw material.** You film every day. You publish nothing. You learn how platforms work, find your thread, start capturing ideas, and get used to the camera.
* **Week 3: technique and first posts.** You set up your phone, light and sound, edit, and publish your first three reels.
* **Week 4: learn from your own data.** You read your analytics, run small experiments, and look at the business side: monetization, disclosure rules, pricing.

Each lesson has the same shape: a goal, an explanation, steps, an example, common mistakes, an exercise and a checklist. The exercises are the course. If you only read, you will have learned about reels, not how to make them.

## The ground rules

1. **A streak beats quality.** On a bad day, record a voice note. That counts. A broken streak is the real enemy.
2. **No publishing in weeks 1-2.** This removes the pressure that makes people freeze. Everything you film in the first two weeks is practice.
3. **One change at a time.** When something is not working, change one thing (light, hook, length), not five.
4. **Your own data beats generic advice.** After you publish, your analytics tell you more than any rule in this course.
5. **Copy structures, not content.** You will study other people's videos to learn patterns. You will never reuse their words, footage or music without rights.
6. **You decide what is public.** Lesson 2.3 covers privacy. Until you have read it, do not publish.

## What you need

You do not need to buy anything to start.

* A smartphone with a decent camera. This course uses an iPhone as the example, and the principles apply to any phone.
* A place to put notes. Anything works: a notes app, a folder, a markdown file. You will use it as an "idea inbox" starting in lesson 3.1.
* Storage: video eats space. Check you have a few gigabytes free before you begin.
* A computer helps for editing but is not required. Phone editors work.
* A tracker. A paper calendar with an X for each day is enough. The repository also ships a checklist you can copy.

Things that are nice later but not needed now: a lavalier microphone, a small tripod, a window with good light, a ring light.

## Set up your tracking

Create a simple tracker with one row per day and columns for: voice note, take filmed, lesson done, published (from week 3). Keep it where you will see it daily. The point is the visible streak.

Decide your **minimum day** now, while you are motivated. Suggested: *one voice note of any length*. On any day when you cannot do more, you do that and stop.

## Do the first take

Record a 30-second video on your phone, in vertical mode, answering this question: *"What do I want from making reels?"* Rules:

* One take. Do not re-record.
* Do not watch it back today.
* Do not show it to anyone.
* Delete it or keep it private.

Why it works: you have now broken the seal. The first take is the highest barrier. Everything after is easier.

## Common mistakes

* **Waiting for the perfect setup.** You will not get one. The set-up improves over weeks.
* **Skipping the first take "until tomorrow".** That is how the habit dies.
* **Planning to publish early.** You are not ready, and it is not the goal.
* **Making the minimum day too big.** If your minimum day takes 30 minutes, you will skip it. Make it five.

## Daily practice

* **Minimum (keeps the streak):** a voice note of any length.
* **Today's take (about 30 seconds):** Your first take from this lesson counts as today's take. From tomorrow, any topic.
* Weeks 1-2 everything stays private; from week 3 follow your publishing plan.
* Don't watch the take right away (lesson 4.1). Tick the day in your tracker.

## Checklist

* [ ] I know the three phases of the course.
* [ ] I have a tracker and a defined minimum day.
* [ ] I have enough free storage.
* [ ] I recorded and did not watch my first 30-second take.
* [ ] I recorded today's voice note and take.
