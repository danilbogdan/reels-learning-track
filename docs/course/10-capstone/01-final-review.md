# Lesson 10.1: Final review and the next 90 days

**English** | [Русский](../../ru/course/10-capstone/01-final-review.md)

**Time:** 90 minutes

## Goal

Look back at your 30 days with an honest eye, record what you learned, and set a plan for the next 90 days that you can actually keep.

## Part 1: The retrospective

Do this in one sitting with your tracker, your analytics table, your experiment log and your first and latest takes. Answer in writing.

### Habit

* How many of the 30 days did I do at least the minimum?
* What was my longest streak?
* What broke the streak, and what would have saved it?

### Skills

Rate yourself 1-5 now, and think back to where you were on day 1.

| Skill | Day 1 | Day 30 |
|---|---|---|
| Capturing ideas | | |
| Writing hooks | | |
| Speaking to the lens | | |
| Light and sound | | |
| Editing speed | | |
| Reading analytics | | |

Pick the **one** with the least progress and the **one** with the most. Write why.

### Output

* How many reels did I film? Publish?
* Which reel am I proudest of? Why?
* Which reel taught me the most?

### Data

* What are my median hook retention and completion rate?
* Which hook types and formats did best?
* Which pillar produced the most shares or follows?
* What did my experiments teach me? (Use the experiment log.)

### Feelings

* What did I enjoy? What did I dread?
* What would I stop doing?
* What did I learn about myself?

## Part 2: The comparison reel (optional)

Watch your day-1 take and your best recent take back to back. If you wish, make a short private (or public, if you decide) comparison video. Seeing the change is a strong motivator.

## Part 3: Decide what you are doing

After 30 days you have enough information to choose. Pick one:

* **Continue as is.** The pace, thread and workload feel right.
* **Adjust.** Keep the habit but change the thread, format, pace or platform.
* **Pause deliberately.** Take a planned break of a defined length, keep the minimum day, and set a date to decide.
* **Stop.** If it isn't giving you enough, stopping is a valid, informed choice. You keep the skills.

Write your choice and one sentence explaining it.

### Checkpoint: after 30 published reels

If you have published 30 reels by now, run the [30-reel checkpoint](../../plan.md#checkpoint-after-30-reels) before choosing. If you have fewer, schedule it for the day you publish number 30 and put it in your calendar. The checkpoint decides whether the next step is more of the same, better quality, a partner, or a producer. Add the content type (expert, personal, reach, selling; lesson 2.2) and the format to each row, so you can see whether one type beats the rest.

## Part 4: The 90-day plan

Break 90 days into three 30-day blocks, each with a theme.

### Block 1 (days 31-60): Consolidate

* Pick a posting frequency you can sustain (for example, two reels a week).
* Keep the weekly rhythm (lesson 8.1).
* Run one experiment per week (lesson 7.3).
* Build the content bank: two finished reels in reserve.
* Goal: 8-10 reels published with stable quality.

### Block 2 (days 61-90): Deepen

* Choose a series or recurring format that fits your thread.
* Try one collaboration (lesson 8.2).
* Improve one production element: sound, light or editing speed.
* Start exploring one monetization model with a small test (lesson 9.1).
* Goal: a recognizable series and your first signal of what your audience values.

### Block 3 (days 91-120, if you continue): Expand

* Review the data of the past 60 days.
* Decide whether to add a platform or a long-form format.
* Revisit your thread statement.
* Revisit pricing and your disclosure policy before taking any deal.

## Part 5: Keep the system

Make a short list of what you will keep doing weekly:

1. Weekly review (30 minutes).
2. Voice notes (daily minimum).
3. One batch filming session.
4. Analytics check at 24 hours and 7 days.
5. Idea inbox harvest.
6. One experiment.

## Part 6: Celebrate

You made a habit and a skill. Whether or not your reels went wide, you now know how to make them, and that is what the course was for. Mark the day. Tell someone. Do something you enjoy.

## Common mistakes at the end

* **Judging 30 days by view counts alone.**
* **Quitting after one flat week.**
* **Making a plan you cannot hold.**
* **Skipping the retrospective.**
* **Forgetting to celebrate.**

## Exercise

1. Complete the retrospective in writing.
2. Choose continue, adjust, pause or stop.
3. Write the 90-day plan with the three blocks.
4. Schedule your next weekly review and put it in your calendar.
5. Note the date of your 30-reel checkpoint.

## Daily practice

* **Minimum (keeps the streak):** a voice note of any length.
* **Today's take (about 30 seconds):** Tell the lens your honest 30-day summary: what changed, what didn't.
* Weeks 1-2 everything stays private; from week 3 follow your publishing plan.
* Don't watch the take right away (lesson 4.1). Tick the day in your tracker.
