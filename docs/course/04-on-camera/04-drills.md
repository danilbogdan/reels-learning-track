# Lesson 4.4: Drills for natural delivery

**English** | [Русский](../../ru/course/04-on-camera/04-drills.md)

**Time:** 20 minutes per session, repeated through the course

## Goal

Build a set of short, repeatable exercises that keep improving your delivery through the course and after.

## How to use drills

Drills are not content. They are practice reps. Film them and watch them privately. Use two or three per day, 10-15 minutes total. Rotate so you do not get bored.

Always follow the same cycle: **do, review in batch, change one thing, do again.**

## The drill set

### Drill 1: Thirty seconds, no prep

Pick a random subject (an object in the room, a past meal, a place you visited) and talk about it for 30 seconds without stopping. Goal: build the habit of continuing when you lose your thread. Don't aim for good; aim for continuous.

### Drill 2: One sentence, ten ways

Take one sentence, say it ten times with ten different feelings: bored, thrilled, secretive, angry, amused, tired, certain, doubtful, kind, urgent. Then pick the version that feels like you. This stretches your range and reveals your natural energy.

### Drill 3: Explain it to a ten-year-old

Choose something you know well and explain it in plain words in 30 seconds. Removing jargon is a core skill for short video.

### Drill 4: Hook sprint

Set a timer for five minutes. Film as many different hooks for one idea as you can. Keep the best two in the idea inbox. This builds fast writing and delivery under mild pressure.

### Drill 5: Story in three beats

Tell a story from your week in three beats: *what happened, what went wrong, what I learned*. Ten seconds each. This trains structure.

### Drill 6: The mirror reversal

Film a 20-second take, then watch it with the sound off and the sound-only separately. Which communicates better, the face or the voice? Work on the weaker.

### Drill 7: The pause drill

Say a sentence, then pause for two full seconds in silence while looking at the lens, then continue. This gets you comfortable with silence, which you can later trim to half a second.

### Drill 8: The slow-fast drill

Record a 20-second take at half speed, then double speed, then the middle. This sets your range for pace.

### Drill 9: Movement

Record a 20-second take while walking. Then one while standing still. Then one while seated. Compare and note which suits which type of reel.

### Drill 10: Retake of a reel

Take your worst take from the past week. Re-record it with one change. This shows the power of iteration.

## A seven-day rotation

| Day | Drills |
|---|---|
| 1 | 1, 2 |
| 2 | 3, 4 |
| 3 | 5, 7 |
| 4 | 6, 8 |
| 5 | 9, 1 |
| 6 | 10, 2 |
| 7 | free choice + weekly review |

## Weekly review

Once a week, watch the best and worst take, side by side, and write:

1. One thing that improved.
2. One thing that did not.
3. One drill to emphasize next week.

## Record your progress

Keep a note or a column in your tracker with the date and one sentence about each session. After a month you will have written proof that you improved, which is the best antidote to discouragement.

## When you feel stuck

* **Shorten the drill** to 15 seconds.
* **Do a voice note only** and still count the day.
* **Change the setting:** film in another room or outside.
* **Take a day of rest** and return. A pause is better than a spiral.

## Common mistakes

* **Drilling the same thing forever.** Rotate.
* **Drilling without reviewing.** Reps without reflection plateau.
* **Treating drills as content.** They are not meant to be posted.
* **Perfectionism.** The aim is trying, not nailing.

## Exercise

Follow the seven-day rotation this week. At the end, write your three observations. Repeat with a different set of drills for the next week.

## Daily practice

* **Minimum (keeps the streak):** a voice note of any length.
* **Today's take (about 30 seconds):** Film two drills from the seven-day rotation.
* Weeks 1-2 everything stays private; from week 3 follow your publishing plan.
* Don't watch the take right away (lesson 4.1). Tick the day in your tracker.

## Checklist

* [ ] I know the 10 drills.
* [ ] I have a 7-day rotation.
* [ ] I do a weekly side-by-side review.
* [ ] I keep a short log of progress.
* [ ] I recorded today's voice note and take.
