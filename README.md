# Reels Learning Track

**English** | [Русский](README.ru.md)

A learning track for getting good at short vertical video about yourself (Instagram Reels, TikTok) without quitting after a week.

The idea is simple: habit first, then technique, then publishing and review.

## Principles

1. A streak matters more than quality. On a bad day a voice note is enough.
2. No publishing for the first two weeks. The goal is a habit and a stock of raw material.
3. Take ideas from other people's successful videos: break down the structure and rework it with your own story. Do not copy.
4. Decide from your own analytics (retention, completion, shares), not from generic advice.
5. Monetize only after views are stable.

## The weeks in two lines each

* **Week 1 :** 7 days in a row, a note plus one 30-second take.
* **Week 2 :** hooks and camera angles, a table of 20 analyzed videos.
* **Week 3 :** first three posts.
* **Week 4 :** 4-5 posts, analytics review, a conclusion about hooks.

## Tooling

The companion repo [reel-skills](https://github.com/danilbogdan/reel-skills) holds reusable skills for making reels from your own footage and notes: analyzing reference videos and linting hooks, cutting pauses and rendering, and digesting voice notes. This repo is the learning plan; reel-skills is the toolbox. See [docs/plan.md](docs/plan.md#tooling) for where each fits.
